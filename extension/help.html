Help & privacy

Open pages and saved work

The sidebar shows eligible open web and file pages. Click a page to switch to it. Use Shift-click to select a range, or Ctrl-click (Cmd-click on Mac) to toggle individual pages. Checkboxes work with the keyboard too.

Drag selected tabs or an open group header into a collection to save them. The tray beside Open tabs shows the same Stash popover as the switcher: save into a new collection and optionally close tabs after saving. Or choose and switch to new collection to adopt this window without reopening tabs. These choices are mutually exclusive; adopting requires all unpinned tabs in this window. With AI configured, a short descriptive name and time appear automatically after saving; otherwise the name is Saved on plus the time. Pinned tabs stay open.

Spaces contain collections. Use the top plus to add a space and the final New collection row to create a collection. Names are edited inline; Enter saves and Escape cancels renaming. Collections contain links, optional groups and a continuation note. Use a collection's expand button to focus it, and Restore to return to the board. The chevron folds the collection. Open adds fresh tabs to this window; New window opens them separately. Every saved page opens as a fresh tab. Use Select to open only some links.

Clicking an individual saved link always opens a new browser tab, including links found through collection search. Drag a saved group header to move the whole group. Moving the last link out removes the emptied group. Hover or focus a note icon to read it; click to keep it open, and press Escape or Close to dismiss it.

Switching always saves Timeline recovery. With no active collection, Save current tabs as a new collection is unchecked by default. With an active collection, Update its name with current tabs is unchecked by default; checking it updates that collection once without enabling auto-update.

Group and sort

Choose Group & sort in the Open tabs … menu. Neo uses built-in defaults to group tabs by website, names them locally and sorts groups and tabs alphabetically. No AI connection is needed. Use the Group panel to choose whether to include existing groups, then apply. Pinned tabs stay pinned. An existing selection limits the action to those tabs in this window. Undo restores the previous browser grouping, colours and order.

Grouping works automatically with built-in website defaults. Group colours are chosen when creating groups and kept on subsequent runs. The Group panel remembers whether to include already grouped tabs. Auto-group new tabs controls local ongoing grouping independently from collection auto-update. Manual corrections and Undo are respected. New automatic groups require at least two matching tabs. Turn Auto-group off in the sidebar. Drag open tabs onto another group or onto the Ungroup drop area to change their membership, with Undo. Web Store pages are saved and grouped like other web pages.

AI tools

Organise collection with AI creates a useful name, a brief collection overview and topic groups in one action. ChatGPT and Gemini belong together in AI chatbots; isolated links stay ungrouped. The overview uses titles, URLs and existing notes rather than page contents. Undo restores the collection and, when tracking, its browser layout.

Group open tabs by topic sends one batch of titles and URLs to your configured provider, applies the groups directly and offers Undo. Cancel stops a pending request; results are rejected if the tabs changed while it was running. AI response times depend on the provider. Individual names remain directly editable without an AI suggestion workflow.

AI organisation works on open tabs or one collection. New saves are named automatically; drag-created collections offer optional filing suggestions. Research overview reads selected open pages and produces a source-cited draft for review. Grouping handles up to 300 links, and research overview up to 20 open pages. Closed or inaccessible pages are reported as unread. Configure your provider under Settings → AI connection.

Type neo followed by Tab or Space in the address bar, then enter a search or press Enter for Library. Settings also offers Open Library in its own window.

Save and switch work

Stash saves to a new or selected existing collection, with an option to close the saved tabs. It pauses the affected current collection before closing tabs. Switch to collection makes that collection current in this window. Leave Save current tabs checked to retain the outgoing session for returning to it, or clear it to skip that retained session. Timeline recovery remains available. Pinned tabs stay open; new tabs and settings pages close without being saved. Close current collection is in the coloured header control. Close all in the main current-window area works even when no collection is active. Remove a saved link with its hover × button; Undo restores it. The broom beside Open tabs shows how many duplicate tabs it will close.

Auto-update follows changes to the current window when On and freezes the saved list when Paused. Settings → Auto-update all collections applies On/Off globally to existing, open and new collections. Use the current collection's switch to pause or resume it. Its colour also appears in the library logo, page icon and toolbar icon. Collection options include a custom colour picker.

Two ways to find your place

The toolbar button opens the library. Alt+Q opens a floating, translucent switcher over the current web page. Its light or dark theme follows your Neo settings. Chrome's protected internal pages use a bounded extension window instead. A group tile opens that group's pages. Real previews appear when a captured image is available; Neo does not visit background pages to manufacture previews.

Each saved collection also has Select: choose individual links or whole groups, then Group, Ungroup, Rename group, Move, Open, or Remove. These edits offer Undo. Choose Select in the switcher to manage live tabs. Click tabs or whole groups to select them, then Group, Ungroup, Rename group, or Close tabs. New groups are named inline. Pinned tabs stay open. Escape exits selection mode first; press it again to go back or close the switcher.

Alt+Shift+K opens search only, without preview cards or the collection dock. Search finds open, saved and recently closed pages, plus notes. Type @ to select a collection or / to choose an action. Up/Down changes the highlighted result; Enter performs its displayed action. Escape clears the query or closes search.

The switcher uses the same @ scopes and / commands. A selected collection lists only its saved links, even when extra copies of those pages are open or recently closed.

Default shortcuts are Alt+Q (switcher), Alt+Shift+K (search only) and Alt+Shift+L (library). Configure shortcuts in the browser's extension settings if another application uses them.

Portable collections

Import accepts Neo JSON, supported Toby collections/cards JSON, bookmark HTML, Markdown links and OneTab text. Review the contents before importing. Imported collections become separate copies.

A library backup includes spaces, current collections, groups, notes, preferences, domain rules and a reference log of recent actions. It excludes API keys, page previews, browser history, tab/session IDs and older recovery snapshots. Imported log entries are informational and never replay browser actions. Restoring preferences is optional.

Markdown and bookmark HTML exports preserve useful structure without requiring Neo. The Markdown export creates a portable file for other note apps. For a large collection, use a Markdown file. Direct Notion export creates a snapshot under a page you review. Share that parent page with your Notion integration first.

Data on this device

Neo's core uses IndexedDB and extension-local storage. It has no required account, backend, analytics or telemetry. Stored links and notes are not automatically uploaded. Chrome's favicon service supplies small site icons.

Optional automatic previews capture only the active page while browsing, exclude private windows, expire after 14 days and use a cache capped at 50 MB. A toolbar action may capture the current web page with temporary active-tab access. Clear previews or revoke website access in Settings.

Saved keys stay in trusted extension-local storage, outside backups. They are not protected by an operating-system keychain. Forget them in Settings when no longer needed.

Optional connections

Collection plans and automatic organisation use link titles, URLs, groups and notes. Space plans receive the chosen space's collections; All spaces must be chosen explicitly. Destination suggestions use existing collection names, notes and sample URLs. Research overview additionally reads the selected open pages after optional site access is granted. Page text is sent to your configured provider only for that overview. Screenshots and browser history are not sent. Review plans and drafts before applying them; stale plans require regeneration.

Notion receives the collection you select for export. People with access to the destination may see it. Large exports use batches; you can pause between batches and continue through Recovery. If a response is lost, Neo marks that batch uncertain and will not blindly resend it.

Bookmark and browser-history access are optional permissions. History search is explicit and limited to the previous 30 days. Revoking optional access stops these connections until you grant it again.

Recovery and limits

A durable snapshot is saved before tabs close. Tabs that navigate, become pinned or otherwise change during the operation are left open. Recovery can reopen saved URLs and groups, but cannot restore unsaved form contents, login state or an application's memory.

Undo reverses the latest compatible action. If newer edits exist, restore an older collection as a separate copy. The last 20 completed action records and up to 1,000 closed-page records from 30 days are retained; unfinished operations remain visible. An interrupted close is never automatically replayed.

Exports to other apps are separate actions and cannot be undone through local library Undo. Browser-internal and other extensions' pages are excluded from saved tab operations. Export a backup before uninstalling: uninstalling removes Neo's local data.
